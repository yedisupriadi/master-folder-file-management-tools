# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and harassment-free project. Constructive disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, discriminatory language, intimidation, doxxing, and publication of confidential information are not.

Contributors should:

- focus feedback on the work and its impact;
- assume good intent while remaining clear about risks and evidence;
- respect project, customer, and document confidentiality;
- accept maintainer decisions about scope and repository safety.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal or customer information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces.
